For archival enquiries relating to the collection, please purchase this product.

Complete the form below with details of your enquiry, and we will search the archive for relevant material. Findings will be sent to you by email.

If you require hard copies, an additional fee will apply, depending on the length of the documents and postage costs. We will confirm any additional charges with you by email before proceeding.

We aim to respond to all research enquiries within 60 days of receiving payment.

We will do our best to locate the information you are looking for. If it is clear that we do not hold the relevant items or documents, we will let you know and issue a refund. If we are unable to find the specific information requested but identify related material that may be of interest, we will contact you to discuss the findings.
