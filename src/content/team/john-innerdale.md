**John Innerdale** is a British architect, landscape artist, and lifelong mountaineer based in the Lake District. An active climber with decades of experience on British rock and international alpine terrain, Innerdale combined his deep passion for mountain exploration with heritage preservation as a co-founder of the **Mountain Heritage Trust (MHT)** alongside Sir Chris Bonington, John Porter, and other prominent figures.

Established to safeguard Britain’s rich climbing legacy, MHT collects, documents, and preserves historic expedition archives, photographs, and mountaineering artifacts. Innerdale served as a curator, trustee, and Patron of the Trust, devoting years of work to keeping the stories, art, and heritage of British mountaineering accessible for future generations.
