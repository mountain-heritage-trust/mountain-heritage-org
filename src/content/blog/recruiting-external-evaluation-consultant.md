The Mountain Heritage Trust is seeking an experienced **Evaluation Consultant** to help us measure, understand and demonstrate the impact of our National Lottery Heritage Fund-supported project, _Moving Mountains with Purpose_.

The Mountain Heritage Trust protects, preserves and shares an internationally significant collection relating to the history of British climbing and mountaineering. Based at Blencathra in the Lake District, we are embarking on an ambitious two-year project to strengthen the Trust, increase access to our collections, grow and diversify our audiences, develop our volunteers and partnerships, and build greater organisational and financial resilience.

We are looking for an evaluator who can work collaboratively with our trustees, staff, volunteers and partners to establish a practical evaluation framework at an early stage of the project and support us in using it throughout delivery.

The commission will include developing qualitative and quantitative measures; establishing a baseline against the project's agreed outcomes and KPIs; advising the team on proportionate and effective methods of capturing evidence; and providing mid-project and final evaluation and learning. 

We are particularly interested in hearing from consultants with experience of evaluating **heritage, cultural, charitable or community projects**, ideally including projects supported by the National Lottery Heritage Fund.

The total budget available is **£5,000 including VAT**. 

**How to apply**

Please submit a CV (2-sides) and short proposal (no more than 4 sides), setting out:

- your proposed approach and methodology; 
- relevant experience, including examples or case studies of similar work; 
- the anticipated number of days/hours required; 
- your daily rate and overall cost; 
- confirmation of your availability to meet the project timetable; and 
- details of two referees. 

Full details of the commission and deliverables are contained in the **External Evaluation Consultant Brief** [https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AL0UZCCjVbw4_7AHhWYtvFwQPLYYI4eu/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=101805274057994467677&rtpof=true&sd=true.](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AL0UZCCjVbw4_7AHhWYtvFwQPLYYI4eu/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=101805274057994467677&rtpof=true&sd=true)

Please submit proposals to [**enquiries@mountain-heritage.org**](mailto:enquiries@mountain-heritage.org) by **17 October 2026**.
