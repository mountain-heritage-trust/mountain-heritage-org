We've loaned a selection of climbing gear and an original Abraham Brothers camera to Rheged for the Forty Fells exhibition.

Forty Fells, a beautiful coffee table book, explores Forty Fells in and around the Lake District and shows the rich heritage and cultural complexity of the area. With mass trespasses, regenerative farming, mine exploration and mountaineering being a small selection of what is represented, it is a genuine window into the soul of the Lakes.

Photographer Tom McNally and author David Felton came together to create the book, and subsequently teamed up with Rheged, nestled in the Northern Lakes, to create the exhibition full of high-quality photos, 3D objects and films to bring their book to life.

We're delighted to be able to provide some mountaineering equipment and the camera to add depth to the photos, with Tom explaining that the Abraham Brothers were a great inspiration in his photographic life.

David and Tom will be at Rheged on 30th October at 7pm for an illustrated talk, make sure you don't miss it!

The exhibition is free to enter and closes on 24th January 2027.
