The Mountain Heritage Trust is seeking an experienced **Fundraising & Income Generation Consultant** to help us strengthen our financial resilience and develop new and sustainable sources of income.

The Mountain Heritage Trust protects, preserves and shares an internationally significant collection relating to the history of British climbing and mountaineering. Based at Blencathra in the Lake District, we are embarking on an ambitious two-year project, _Moving Mountains with Purpose_, supported by the National Lottery Heritage Fund.

As part of this work, we have recently appointed a new Communications & Business Development Manager and are looking for an experienced consultant to work alongside them and the Trust to develop a practical **Fundraising & Income Generation Strategy and Action Plan**.

The commission will include reviewing MHT's current position and opportunities; identifying realistic short, medium and longer-term income streams; developing a practical strategy and action plan; creating tools, resources and a prospect pipeline for fundraising and corporate support; and providing practical advice and mentoring as the strategy moves into delivery.

We expect the substantive **strategy development and mobilisation phase to be completed by April 2027**, with a proportion of the available consultancy resource retained to provide **ongoing mentoring and implementation support through to September 2028**.

We are particularly interested in hearing from consultants with a strong track record in **fundraising, income diversification and organisational sustainability**, ideally within the heritage, cultural, charitable or related sectors. Experience of developing corporate partnerships, sponsorship, trusts and foundations and individual giving would be particularly relevant.

The total budget available for the commission is **£10,000 including VAT and expenses**.

**How to apply**

Please submit a proposal of **no more than four sides of A4 (excluding CVs and references)** setting out:

- your proposed approach and methodology; 
- relevant experience, including brief examples of similar work; 
- anticipated number of days/hours; 
- how you propose to allocate your consultancy time between strategy development and subsequent mentoring/implementation support; 
- your daily rate and total cost; and 
- confirmation of your availability to meet the proposed timetable. 

Full details of the commission, deliverables and assessment criteria are contained in the **Fundraising & Income Generation Consultant Brief** [https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Eca2xfL_XnOvmHk5yj8AERS15xaYUjDx/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=101805274057994467677&rtpof=true&sd=true](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Eca2xfL_XnOvmHk5yj8AERS15xaYUjDx/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=101805274057994467677&rtpof=true&sd=true).

Please submit proposals to [**enquiries@mountain-heritage.org**](mailto:enquiries@mountain-heritage.org) by **17 October 2026**, using **“Fundraising & Income Generation Consultant Proposal”** in the subject line.
