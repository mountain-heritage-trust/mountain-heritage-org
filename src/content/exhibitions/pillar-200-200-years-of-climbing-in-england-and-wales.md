On the 31st October, we’ll be opening our Pillar 200 full exhibition at the Beacon Museum, Whitehaven. The exhibition will follow climbing in England and Wales from the first ascent of Old West Route by shepherd John Atkinson, the first time a route was tackled simply for the challenge of climbing.

Using material from our collection, the Fell and Rock Climbing Club, guidebook writer and Needle Sports founder Stephen Reid, the DMM Climbing archives, and the Abraham Brothers, we’ll explore climbing's heritage through photographs and stories, alongside a selection of gear from different eras.

This is the first time the Lake District based museum will host a climbing and mountaineering focussed exhibition, where we’ll also have material from the local Mountain Rescue team, Cockermouth.

This exhibition has been generously supported by DMM Climbing, The National Lottery Heritage Fund, Mountain Rescue England and Wales, Needle Sports, Fell and Rock Climbing Club, Outside, Beta Climbing Designs, Joe Brown's Shops, UKClimbing.com, The Climbers Shop and the BMC.
